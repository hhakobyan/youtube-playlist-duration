# Privacy Policy — Playlist Duration & Controls for YouTube

_Last updated: 2026-11-11_

This extension does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

- It runs only on `https://www.youtube.com/*` pages.
- It reads the video durations shown in the playlist panel on the current page, adds them
  up in your browser, and displays the total next to the playlist position counter.
- When playback is paused or resumed with earphones or media keys, it asks the YouTube
  player on the page to show its controls and play/pause icon.
- It makes no network requests and uses no analytics or tracking.
- The only thing it stores is your on/off choice for each feature, saved with
  `chrome.storage.sync` (your browser's extension storage, which your browser may sync
  across your own signed-in devices). No other data is stored.

If you have questions, open an issue in this project's GitHub repository.
